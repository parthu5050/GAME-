KNOW MORE ABOUT PAH TUM ▼
Pah Tum is an engaging strategic board game played on a 7x7 grid. Players take turns placing marks to form contiguous lines of matching symbols (lengths of 3, 4, 5, 6, or 7+) while strategically blocking their opponents to maximize score and secure victory!
